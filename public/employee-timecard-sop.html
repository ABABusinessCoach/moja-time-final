How to Review & Update Your Timecard

Employee Standard Operating Procedure - Moja Behavioral Services

Employee Guide
Receive Email → Enter PIN → Review Hours → Add Notes or Questions → Approve
Deadline: You must review your timecard by 8:00 PM EST on the day you receive the email. If you do not respond and have no pending corrections, your timecard will be auto-approved as-is.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1 Open the Timecard Link from Your Email

You will receive an email with the subject "Your Timecard is Ready for Review" at the end of each pay period (every other Friday).

Click the "Review My Timecard" button in the email. This will open the timecard review page in your browser.

2 Enter Your 4-Digit PIN

You will be asked to enter your 4-digit PIN -- the same one you use to clock in and out each day.

Type your PIN into the boxes. The system will verify it automatically after you enter the 4th digit.

Forgot your PIN? Contact your manager to have it reset. Do not share your PIN with other employees.
3 Review Your Timecard

You will see a table showing your full 2-week pay period with:

  • Each day's shift -- clock-in time, clock-out time
  • Breaks -- duration of each
  • Regular hours (Reg column)
  • Overtime hours (OT column) -- anything over 40 hours per week
  • Weekly and pay period totals at the bottom

Look at each day carefully. Make sure the clock-in and clock-out times match when you actually arrived and left.

If Your Hours Are Correct

4a Tap "Approve" to Confirm

If everything looks correct, scroll to the bottom and tap the green "Approve" button.

That's it! Your timecard is confirmed and will be sent to your manager for final sign-off.

All done! If your hours are correct, the whole process takes less than 1 minute. Just verify and approve.

If Your Hours Need a Correction

4b Tap "Add Notes or Questions"

If something looks wrong, tap the orange "Add Notes or Questions" button at the bottom of your timecard. A pop-up window will appear where you can flag the issue and propose a correction.

You will have two options for how to correct the shift:

Option When to Use What You Enter
Override Hours You know the total hours you worked that day Hours and minutes (e.g., 8:30 for 8 hours 30 minutes)
Edit Times You know the correct clock-in and/or clock-out time The corrected start and end times
5 Enter the Correct Information

If using "Override Hours": Type the total hours and minutes you worked in H:MM format.

  • 8 hours = 8:00
  • 8 hours 15 min = 8:15
  • 8 hours 30 min = 8:30
  • 8 hours 45 min = 8:45

If using "Edit Times": Select the correct clock-in and clock-out times from the time pickers.

6 Provide a Reason (Required)

In the "Reason for edit" text box, briefly explain why the correction is needed. Be specific so your manager can approve quickly.

Good examples:

  • "Forgot to clock out, actually left at 5:00 PM"
  • "Clock-in didn't register, arrived at 7:00 AM (verified with manager)"
  • "System showed wrong time -- was here until 4:30, not 3:30"
  • "Worked through break, did not take 15-min break"
7 Tap "Submit for Approval"

Click the blue "Submit for Approval" button. Your correction request is now sent to your manager.

You will see a yellow Pending badge appear on that shift. This means your request is waiting for manager approval.

8 Wait for Manager Decision, Then Approve

Your manager will review and either approve or reject your correction:

  • Approved -- Your hours are updated. The corrected time is now reflected in your total.
  • Rejected -- The original hours remain. You'll see the manager's reason. You may submit a new correction if needed.

Once all your corrections are resolved, return to the timecard and tap "Approve" to finalize.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I miss the 8 PM deadline?

If you haven't added any notes or corrections, your timecard will auto-approve with the hours as shown. If you notice an error after this, contact your manager directly -- they can still make edits on their end.

Can I correct more than one day?

Yes. Click on each shift that needs fixing and submit a correction for each one individually. You can have multiple pending corrections at the same time.

Can I change my correction after submitting?

Yes, as long as your manager hasn't reviewed it yet. You can withdraw a pending correction and submit a new one.

What counts as overtime?

Any hours over 40 in a single work week (Saturday through Friday) are counted as overtime. The system calculates this automatically per week, not per pay period.

Are breaks deducted from my hours?

No. All breaks are paid and are included in your total hours. You do not need to worry about break time being subtracted from your paycheck.

I see a shift I don't recognize. What do I do?

Click on that shift and submit a correction with a note explaining you did not work that time. Your manager will investigate and correct it.
